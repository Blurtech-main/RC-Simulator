Modifications made for RC Trainer:

- scene transforms baked into the runtime mesh;
- coordinates reoriented to +X right wing, +Y up, +Z forward;
- dimension-calibrated to the official 11.00 m wingspan, 8.28 m length, and
  2.72 m height at 1:6.39535 scale: 1.720 m x 1.294 m x 0.425 m;
- all six base-colour material maps converted to embedded full-resolution
  2048 x 2048 RGBA runtime maps without texture downscaling;
- original 33 MB GLB retained unchanged as `cessna-172-original.glb`.
